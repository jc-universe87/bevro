# Trademark and brand

The **Apache-2.0 licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the software**. It does
not grant rights to the Bevro name, logo or brand assets — the files under
`brand/` and `web/public/brand/`.

**You may** use the name and logo to refer to this project: writing about it,
linking to it, saying that your tool works with Bevro, keeping the branding in
place when you run your own copy, or including a screenshot.

**Please do not** use the name or logo as the branding of your own product,
service or fork, or in a way that suggests the Bevro authors endorse,
maintain or support it. If you fork Bevro and distribute it as something of
your own, give it your own name and your own logo.

Not sure whether what you have in mind is fine? Open an issue and ask.
